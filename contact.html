Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping (2–3 days) and overnight options are also available at checkout. International orders may take 10–21 business days depending on the destination.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be in original, unused condition with original packaging. Simply contact our support team and we'll guide you through the return process.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes! We ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates start at $14.99. Customs duties and taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations.
How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your package on our website or directly on the carrier's website.
Are all products covered by warranty?
Most electronics come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 1 to 2 years. Warranty details are listed on each product page. We also offer extended warranty plans on select items.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order may already be processed for shipping. Please contact us immediately if you need to make changes.
